# Light and dark app looks

Every one of the 27 looks supports Light and Dark in the existing App look picker, including welcome, Settings and profile. Brightness is stored separately from the look; existing installs keep each look's original appearance until they choose. Switching brightness never unlocks a paid, event, level, trophy or owner look.

Alternate palettes preserve typography, border geometry and theme pigments. Light versions use tinted paper/plastic surfaces and dark actions; dark versions use shaded surfaces and bright actions. Shared skin roles, legacy palette remapping, native controls, nameplates, picker thumbnails and the browser address bar follow brightness. Each variant has a bundled scene.

The missing Haunted, Winterland, Frozen, Inferno and Glitch scenes now include original local SVG artwork: moonlit pumpkins and bare trees, decorated snowy evergreens, icy peaks, lava/embers and a digital court. Scenes stay behind the interface, without animation or remote asset requests.

Validation: all 54 skin palettes meet 4.5:1 for primary/muted panel text, raised text and action text. Tests also cover brightness persistence, original light remapping and unchanged unlock gates. Event scene contact sheet inspected; full in-browser/mobile layout checks remain outstanding.

## Interface continuation

CourtChat replaces the phone app screen within its existing footprint; Back and Escape restore the app grid and focus. Page pins use compact named rows and remove buttons. Phone paging respects reduced motion. Main-menu footer groups identity and help/community links. Settings now groups appearance/sound, gameplay/accessibility, saves/recovery and app/privacy/support.

82–0 offers a first-run Quick Spin with Rookie difficulty and visible ratings. All original variants and modifiers remain in expandable sections. The simulator and scoring rules are unchanged.

Career autopilot offers 0.6, 2.5 (default) and 6-second reading pauses. Give up simulation cancels the current worker and preserves the prior save. Abandon run confirms deletion and awards no retirement rewards. Cancelled callbacks cannot persist a late result. Career page spacing and progress transitions are calmer.

Lucky and Ultra Lucky support paid ten-roll batches with the full cost checked before any roll; ten free Lucky Spins can also be used together. Existing single-spin odds, duplicate rewards and pity apply sequentially. Mythic Career reveals use the Career rarity for particles, with a magenta treatment and an explicit label. Portrait-frame stripes were removed.

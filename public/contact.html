Contact and support

CourtVision is developed by NineFoldVision. The existing Discord community is the published way to reach the developer, report problems and discuss the game. Discord is an external service and requires its own account to participate.

On this page
  1. Report a bug
  2. Save and account help
  3. Privacy requests
  4. Ideas and gameplay questions

Report a bug

Describe the mode and action that failed, the result you expected and what actually happened. Include your browser, device and whether you were signed in or offline. Exact steps and an error screenshot are more useful than “it does not work.”

  • Note the season, round or screen where the problem happened.
  • Say whether it happens again after reopening the same save.
  • Never post passwords, sign-in links, account tokens or payment information. Backup files contain game data: review them and agree on an appropriate support channel before sharing.

Save and account help

Do not clear browser data when investigating a missing save. First check the same browser and profile, then look for save recovery points or a downloaded backup. The FAQ explains the difference between local saves and cloud progress.

Account deletion is available from the account screen in Community. If it fails, report the error without sharing authentication details. Cancel any recurring Support Shop subscription before deleting the account.

Privacy requests

Read the Privacy Policy for the data disclosures and controls. If you need help reaching the developer about a privacy request, use the Discord community to ask for an appropriate private contact route; do not post personal information in public channels. No response-time guarantee is made.

Ideas and gameplay questions

For a gameplay question, begin with the FAQ, beginner guide or mode guides. When suggesting a change, explain the situation it would improve and which mode it affects. Keeping reports specific helps a solo developer reproduce and prioritize them.

Ready to play? Court Vision is free and runs in your browser. Start a league, or join the Discord.

Getting started

What is CourtVision?

CourtVision is a basketball management and simulation game by NineFoldVision. You make roster, training and coaching decisions, then the simulation plays out possessions and produces results. You can run a franchise, build historical teams for short challenges, or follow one player through a career. Pixel-art games let you watch the action as well as read the statistics.

Is Court Vision free?

Yes. You can play the browser game for free. The web version includes advertising support and an optional Support Shop with passes and supporter bundles; buying something is not required to start playing. Ad availability depends on your settings, connection and whether an ad is served.

How do I start playing?

Open the game and choose a mode on the main menu. Choose Franchise for a league you manage over multiple seasons; its setup includes real historical leagues, generated leagues and challenges. Choose 82-0 Challenge for a focused team-building run, or Career Mode to follow one player. Read the setup rules before starting: each mode has its own goals and options.

For your first franchise, pick a team, inspect its roster, set a rotation and simulate a game before advancing a whole season. The beginner guide walks through trades, the schedule and the offseason.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for local single-player play. An optional account enables cloud progress and online features such as posting results and maintaining a public profile. Those features require an internet connection and an available account service; they are not the same as local saves.

Can I play on my phone?

The game includes mobile layouts and runs in modern phone browsers. Large roster tables may need horizontal scrolling, and longer simulations can take more time on a phone. Try a short challenge first, keep the tab open while it runs, and use a larger screen when you want more room for detailed management.

If Google sign-in fails inside Instagram or another in-app browser, open CourtVision in your normal browser and start sign-in there.

Is downloading required?

No. The browser version starts from the website. Installation is optional: the installation guide explains adding the app to supported devices. A Windows download is also linked there. Your browser and the installed edition may keep separate local saves, so use a backup when moving between them.

Game modes

What game modes are there, and how long do they take?

The seven main menu choices are Franchise, League Hunt, 82-0 Challenge, Career Mode, Survival, Story Mode and Your Career. Franchise includes additional setups such as Rebuild Challenge and All-Time Draft. Some modes offer daily, weekly or difficulty variants.

Short challenges can fit a brief session, while franchise management can continue for many seasons. Actual time depends on your device, how much you watch and how many decisions you make; menu time estimates are not limits.

How does Franchise Mode work?

Manage a team across the season and offseason: set rotations, hire staff, trade, negotiate contracts, scout prospects and draft. Real historical setups and generated leagues offer different starting points. Budgets, roster needs and player development make a strong team a longer-term project than simply collecting high overall ratings.

The Franchise setup also includes Rebuild Challenge, All-Time Draft, Time Machine and World Games options. Check each setup for its rules. Start with How to Play, then use the guides for a particular challenge.

How does Hunt Mode work?

In League Hunt, spin a six-player squad and a coach, lock your choices, and choose a training focus. The standard road contains ten best-of-seven series under historical era rules, with a semi-boss and a final boss. Between series you choose boosts and visit shops at designated stops.

Losing a series costs a life; the starting number depends on difficulty. Spend resources with the next matchup in mind, rather than only raising one star’s rating. Special variants and bonus opponents can change the run. See the League Hunt guide.

What is the 82-0 Challenge?

Build a ten-player team and choose a coach, then try to win all 82 regular-season games and go 16-0 in the playoffs. Setup choices include quick spins, franchise-and-era selections and category-based drafting. A team of scorers still needs defense, rebounding and a workable rotation.

Daily and other variants have their own setup rules. Results and records let you compare runs even when you fall short of perfection. Read the 82-0 guide.

How does Career Mode work?

Create a player through the historical-player wheel or the MyPlayer builder, then follow his seasons, training, team changes, awards and retirement. Wheel choices combine different aspects of real players into your created player; they do not copy one historical career exactly.

Career Mode summarizes a whole playing career and evaluates its legacy. It is separate from Your Career, which starts younger and focuses on season-by-season life and basketball choices. See the Career Mode guide.

How does Your Career Mode work?

Start a player at age 13 and choose his identity, position and playing style. Decisions shape training and the route through youth basketball, school, college and professional opportunities. Later choices include entering the draft and selecting contracts.

Read the options each season: this mode is about shaping a path over time, rather than using the Career Mode wheel to assemble a finished starting build.

How does Survival Mode work?

Choose ten players from the opening deal and designate a Franchise Player who cannot be taken. Play historical opponents one game at a time. After a win, the opponent can claim one of your strongest players and you choose a replacement from its offers. Difficulty changes which players are at risk.

Shields protect against player claims; they do not normally erase a lost game. A loss ends the run. Boss opponents appear every fifth round, so saving a shield and maintaining a balanced rotation matter. The Daily Survival uses a shared seed and disables relic bonuses.

How does Story Mode work?

Street to the League follows a basketball story from the park toward a professional career. Story cards, relationships and decisions connect the games, with different endings depending on the path you take. Games use the simulation engine, while the story gives those results a narrative setting.

Choose Story Mode when you want characters and choices alongside basketball, rather than full control of an entire franchise.

Gameplay systems

How do player ratings and attributes work?

Overall is a summary, not a guarantee of performance. Individual shooting, finishing, passing, defense, rebounding and physical attributes describe different skills. Tendencies influence what a player attempts, while roles, minutes, teammates and opponents affect the opportunities he gets.

Compare attributes relevant to your lineup: a shooter needs spacing and touches; a defensive big can help without leading the scoring. Rating scales and caps differ between modes and special options. The ratings guide explains the main summaries.

How do historical basketball eras work?

Historical franchise setups start with teams and players from the selected season. Era settings and historical challenge matchups can change rules and styles, including three-point availability, pace and defensive conditions. A modern shooting lineup therefore does not play every historical matchup under today’s conditions.

Historical statistics supply inputs, but CourtVision’s ratings and simulated outcomes are its own model. A replay is not a promise to reproduce real standings or careers. Read Real NBA history.

How do draft classes and scouting work?

Franchise drafts let you compare prospects and use scouting information before making a selection. Historical and generated setups provide different prospect pools. Scout estimates of potential are not certain forecasts: current ability, development and your team’s needs all matter.

Inspect the available reports and player details, compare prospects at the positions you need, and keep more than one target in mind before your pick. The highest estimated potential is not automatically the best immediate starter.

How do players develop?

Players change over seasons rather than remaining fixed at their starting rating. Age, potential and development systems affect their progression; training choices let you emphasize aspects of a player’s game. Historical development settings can also influence how real players progress.

Use the player’s current skills and role when choosing training. A promising prospect still needs time, and an older star may decline. The outcome is a simulation, not a guaranteed growth schedule.

Can I watch games and coach?

Yes. The game supports simulated results and a pixel-art Watch Game view. Coaching controls include rotations and tactical decisions; the available controls depend on the mode and screen. Team construction and coaching influence the simulation, but do not guarantee the result of an individual possession.

For franchise management, review the box score as well as the result. Shooting efficiency, turnovers, rebounds and playing time can help explain why a higher-rated roster lost.

What is Sandbox mode?

Sandbox provides editing tools for experimenting with a league, including player editing and importing historical players. It changes the competitive context: leagues marked as using Sandbox do not qualify for the normal GM achievement and Rebuild Challenge checks. Keep a separate save if you want both an experiment and a competitive franchise.

How do relics and rarity work?

The Relic Vault contains Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic relics, plus Secret relics with special abilities. Regular relics contribute luck when equipped in available slots. Duplicate rewards, stacking, upgrades and extra slots are handled by the Vault; simply owning every relic does not equip them all.

Earned rewards and coins can supply more spins. Read the Vault’s current descriptions and odds before spending coins. Shared daily and weekly challenges can disable luck and special abilities to keep their starting conditions comparable.

What are Lucky Spins?

In the Relic Vault, Lucky Spins use a different rarity distribution and exclude Common relics. They can use earned Lucky Spins or the displayed coin price. They improve the distribution; they do not make every result a Mythic or Secret.

The Career wheel also uses the phrase Lucky Spin for its own player-building mechanic. Follow the explanation on the screen you are using: a Vault spin rewards a relic, while a Career wheel choice affects your player build.

What is my GM level?

Your profile accumulates XP from supported completed activities and achievements across modes. Level and achievement rewards include progression and cosmetic unlocks, which you can inspect in the profile and GM Locker. The game checks eligibility, so not every edited or experimental result earns competitive progress.

What is the Mode of the Week?

The menu highlights a featured mode that earns bonus XP. Check the current featured-mode panel before starting a run, because the selection rotates and reward limits apply. Daily goals and achievements offer other objectives when you want something more specific than another season.

How do leaderboards work?

Community boards compare supported results, profile progression and other records. Sign in to associate online submissions with your account. Different boards measure different things: XP, a career legacy score and a challenge result are not interchangeable rankings.

Shared challenges use their own seeds and eligibility rules. A result must sync successfully before it can appear online; a local record alone does not prove that the server received it.

Can I play the same league as a friend?

A league code lets a friend start from the same league setup while playing a separate simulation. Online Leagues are a different feature: signed-in members share a league state through the server. Check the relevant setup and account status before choosing between a parallel challenge and a shared league.

How do ranked seasons and PvP work?

Ranked competition and League Hunt PvP are available through the online community features. Ranked events contribute points under their own rules; Hunt PvP uses a submitted squad against other players’ teams and tracks a rating. These features require account services and differ from a local single-player run. Check the current event and submission screens for eligibility and results.

Saving and technical help

Where are my saves? Can I lose them?

Leagues and in-progress Career Mode worlds are stored locally in the browser, using browser databases and local storage. Clearing site data, using private browsing, changing browser profiles or switching devices can make those saves unavailable.

An account syncs supported profile progress and retired Career Mode records, not a full copy of every league. Use Backup everything in Backups or the GM Locker to download a separate file. A successful cloud sync is not a replacement for a league backup.

Online Leagues are an explicit exception: they share league snapshots through the server. Your Career also syncs its player progress with an account. These features do not turn every local franchise save into a cloud backup.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Download Backup everything on the original device. Open CourtVision on the destination device and choose Restore from a file. Read the confirmation before restoring: matching records can be replaced, while other records remain. Back up the destination device first if it has progress you want to preserve.

Account sign-in can bring supported synced progress across devices, but active leagues and in-progress Career Mode worlds require a backup. Authentication sessions and account passes are not transferred as ordinary backup data.

What should I do if the game crashes?

First reopen CourtVision in the same browser and profile, then check your saved games. Do not clear site data as a first troubleshooting step: that can delete the local save you are trying to recover. A crash before a save completes may lose recent actions.

For a franchise, check the save recovery controls for available restore points. Restore as New League opens a separate recovered save. A downloaded backup is another option. Recovery is only possible if a usable save, restore point or backup exists.

Why does a simulated season take a few seconds?

A season involves many games, each with possessions and player statistics, plus league updates and offseason work. Larger leagues and longer careers require more calculation. The game uses background workers for simulation jobs, but device speed, memory and browser limits still affect completion time.

Keep the tab open while a job runs and avoid repeatedly starting the same action. If it appears stuck, note the mode, season, action and any error message before reopening the game or reporting it.

Can I play offline?

The installed web app can reuse cached files, and the Windows edition is designed for offline play. Open the modes you need while connected first; cached data can be missing or cleared, so offline availability is not guaranteed for every first-time action.

Signing in, cloud sync, online boards and other server-backed features need a connection. Keep a local backup before relying on offline play or switching editions.

I found a bug or have an idea

Use the Contact and support page to reach the existing Discord community. Include your mode, browser, device, what you expected, what happened and the steps that trigger it. A screenshot of an error can help.

Do not post passwords, sign-in links or account tokens. Backup files contain your game data; share one only when needed for diagnosis and after checking what it includes. For recovery steps, see the crash and saving answers above.

Accounts and privacy

How do accounts work?

The web game supports optional Google or Discord sign-in through Supabase Auth. CourtVision uses the provider’s sign-in flow instead of asking you to create a separate game password. A session is kept in your browser so you can stay signed in.

Choose your GM name and check the account’s sync status when moving between devices. On a shared computer, take care which account is signed in: switching accounts can replace the local set of synced profile progress with the other account’s progress.

What information is stored with my account?

The account system uses an account identifier, email and provider details, a GM profile, and supported game progress. Public profiles and boards display information such as your GM name, level, statistics and submitted results. Retired created players can also be public.

Local league saves are separate from cloud profile progress. Optional online features and purchases can process additional records. Read the Privacy Policy for the current disclosures, and avoid putting private information in your public GM name.

If you deliberately create or join an Online League, its shared league state is uploaded to Supabase storage so other members can play it. That is separate from ordinary single-player franchise saves. Your Career also includes account-synced player progress; this differs from an in-progress Career Mode world.

Can I delete my account or request help with my data?

The account screen in Community includes account deletion. It calls the server to delete the account; wait for confirmation rather than assuming a failed request worked. Local progress is separate and is not erased just by deleting the account.

If you have a recurring Support Shop pass, cancel the subscription first: deleting a game account is not the same as cancelling billing. For a privacy request or a deletion problem, use Contact and support.

How are cookies and browser storage used?

The game uses local storage and browser databases for saves, settings and account-related state. Google advertising can use cookies or similar technologies. The web version also integrates host analytics and Umami for usage measurements; these are distinct from saving a league.

Open Cookie Settings in the game to revisit the configured advertising consent options. Existing third-party cookies may need to be removed using browser controls. Clearing all site data also removes local game progress, so download a backup first. See the Privacy Policy.

Does CourtVision display advertisements?

The web version is configured for Google AdSense banner placements. Whether an ad appears depends on consent handling, ad availability, connection, blockers and any active ad-free entitlement. Advertising does not need to load for local gameplay to work. The offline Windows build excludes network ads.

Google’s regional consent message is managed through the site’s advertising configuration. Read the Privacy Policy and use the game’s Cookie Settings for your available choices.

Is Court Vision affiliated with the NBA?

No. CourtVision is an independent game by NineFoldVision, not an official NBA product and not endorsed by the NBA, its teams or players. The About page identifies the historical data sources and distinguishes those statistics from the game’s own ratings and simulation.

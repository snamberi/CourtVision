Is Court Vision free?

Yes. The web version is free and supported by ads. The offline Windows edition is free and has no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without an account, exactly as before. An account is free and optional: sign in with Discord, Google or an email link (no password) from the main menu.

With an account, your GM level, achievements, trophies, League Hunt, 82-0 Challenge, Rebuild and weekly results, daily goals and retired Career Mode players follow you to every device, and you appear on the online leaderboards, ranked seasons and League Hunt PvP. GM leagues and careers still in progress stay on the device where you play them: they are several megabytes each, so use Backup everything to move those.

What game modes are there, and how long do they take?

Seven. Pick by how much time you have:

What is the 82-0 Challenge?

A quick mode about one question: can your team go undefeated? Spin ten players and a coach (Quick Spin), or roll a franchise and an era and pick one player from everyone who played there (Franchise Spin). Then play all 82 games against real teams from every era, including boss teams like the 72-10 Bulls and the 73-9 Warriors, and four playoff rounds. Every run gets a score, there is a Daily 82-0 with the same spins for everyone, and going 82-0 unlocks the Undefeated title and profile frame. See the 82-0 Challenge guide.

What is the Mode of the Week?

Each week one mode (the 82-0 Challenge, League Hunt, Career Mode or the Rebuild Challenge) earns double XP: every run you finish in it that week gives its XP twice, up to 3,000 bonus XP a week. It changes every Monday and is shown on the main menu.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Court Vision runs in any modern phone browser, and you can install it as an app from the main menu (on iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen). The quick modes (82-0 Challenge, Career Mode, League Hunt) are made for short sessions on a phone.

Is Court Vision affiliated with the NBA?

No. Court Vision is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA, its teams or its players. Historical statistics come from Basketball-Reference.com; the ratings are Court Vision's own.

Where are my saves? Can I lose them?

Saves live in your browser's storage. Clearing your browsing data, using a private window, or switching browsers or devices means you won't see them. To keep them safe, download a backup of everything from the GM Locker or from Backups on the main menu. One file holds every league, career, League Hunt record and achievement.

Signed in, your progress (not your GM leagues) is also kept with your account, so clearing this browser no longer loses your level, trophies, records or retired careers.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Download a backup on the old device, open Court Vision on the new one, and choose Restore from a file. Restoring adds and replaces; it never deletes anything already there.

Can I play offline?

Yes, two ways. Install the app: on Chrome, Edge or Android, use the Install app button on the main menu (on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen). After your first visit it opens without a connection, and any mode you have played once keeps working offline.

Or download the Windows edition (also the download button in the top-right corner of the game). It runs entirely offline and has no ads.

Are the players and ratings real?

Real League, League Hunt, Career Mode and Rebuild Challenge use real NBA players and their real statistics. The ratings are Court Vision's own, computed from those statistics. Random leagues use generated players. See Real NBA history.

Why does a simulated season take a few seconds?

Every game is simulated possession by possession by a full engine, around 1,230 games a season plus the playoffs. It runs in background workers so the game stays responsive.

What is Sandbox mode?

Sandbox unlocks tools such as editing any player, God Mode, importing any real player from any era, and sticking players to teams. Leagues where Sandbox has been used no longer count toward GM achievements or the Rebuild Challenge board.

How do ads and privacy work?

The web version shows ads through Google AdSense when your consent choices allow it, and uses cookie-free aggregate analytics, including anonymous counts of which modes are started and finished (never names, save contents or identifiers; nothing is sent when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control). The Privacy Policy explains what is stored and your choices.

Can I play the same league as a friend?

Yes. Every league started in this version has a league code on its dashboard. Send it to a friend; when they type it into Have a league code? on the main menu, they get the exact same starting league: the same rosters, generated players, draft classes and schedule, and the same team if you included it. Games are simulated fresh for each of you, so how the season goes is up to you.

For a weekly competition, play the Rebuild of the Week or the Career of the Week and post your result to the online leaderboard.

What is my GM level?

Your GM Profile level adds up everything you finish in every mode: GM seasons, wins, titles and achievements in official leagues, retired careers and Hall of Famers, League Hunt runs, Rebuild stars, weekly challenges and daily goals. Progress from before levels existed counts too. Levels unlock share-card frames, court floors for Watch Game and titles, which you equip in the GM Locker.

How do ranked seasons and PvP work?

Ranked runs one season per calendar month. The Daily Legend, the Rebuild of the Week, the Career of the Week and your daily goals earn rank points, your best result per event counting, and the total places you in a tier: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond or Legend. Reaching Gold or higher unlocks that GM title for good.

League Hunt PvP: when you finish a League Hunt, that squad (with its coach, training, boosts and items) becomes your PvP team. Challenge other GMs' teams in a best-of-seven under a random era's rules; ratings move by Elo, and your team defends your rating while you are away.

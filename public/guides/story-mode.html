Story Mode guide

Story Mode: Street to the League follows one player from a park in his hometown to the NBA. Scenes ask you to choose who you trust, how you train and what you say. Key games are played by the main game engine with you on the floor, and how you play changes the story.

You also pick a hometown, from Baltimore to Oakland, Chicago's South Side to New Orleans.

The five chapters

  1. The Blacktop (age 16): summer at Carver Park, and a run against the city's top sophomore.
  2. Varsity (17): tryouts, the season opener, a rivalry game, and a shot at the state title.
  3. The Circuit (18): AAU summer, a showcase in Las Vegas, recruiting rankings, and people who want something from you.
  4. The Choice (19): where you play next.
  5. Draft Night (20): the moment everything has been building to.

After the draft, the story continues for fifteen seasons in the real NBA.

The people on the road

Four people walk the road with you: your best friend Dre, Coach Ray from the rec center, your little sister Maya, and your rival Marcus "Ice" Vance. Choices build or strain each relationship. On one Friday night you can go to a party with Dre (more hype, a closer friendship), work out with Coach Ray (better playmaking and more grit, but Dre feels left out), stay in and watch film, or take Maya to the movies as you promised.

Games and training

  • Key games are real simulations with you on the floor. A win or a loss changes the next scene, your hype and your options.
  • Training sessions give you points to spend on your skills before the next stretch of games.
  • Your choices and results raise or lower your hype and grit, which shape what comes next.

Endings

The ending depends on your career, your hype, your grit and the people you kept close. Everything is seeded, so the same choices tell the same story. Make different ones to see where else the road goes.

Related modes

Want more control over a whole career? Your Career starts at 13 with more choices about schools and contracts, and Career Mode builds a player for the full GM game.

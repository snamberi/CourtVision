Survival guide

Survival: Last Team Standing starts you with a stacked team of all-time greats. You play real teams from NBA history one game at a time, each under its own era's rules. Every win has a price: the team you beat claims one of your players. One loss and the run is over.

Build the team

  1. You are dealt 14 great players from all of history, one season each: five guards, five forwards and four centers. At least one legendary season comes at each position; the rest are epic.
  2. Keep ten of them. That is your whole roster.
  3. Name a Franchise Player. He can never be claimed, whatever happens.

Each round

  • You see the next opponent and its rating before tip-off. Opponents are real historical teams, and you never face the same team twice in a run.
  • The game is played under the opponent's era rules, with its era's coaching style.
  • Lose and the run ends. Win and the beaten team claims one of your players (never the Franchise Player). Then you sign one of three of their players to refill your roster.
  • Every fifth round is a boss game against a champion team.

Shields

You start with one Shield, and each boss win adds another, up to three. When a team claims a player you want to keep, spend a Shield: the claim is blocked and they take someone else instead. You can only shield once a round.

Cash and emergency signings

Each win pays 25 cash, and a boss win pays 50. Before a round you can spend 60 on one emergency signing from three free agents, players a little below your team's average who are not already on your roster. It fills an empty spot or replaces your lowest-rated player, never the Franchise Player. One emergency signing per round.

Three levels

RookieSofterOne of your top four, not always the best0.75x

On every level the opposition gets steadily stronger: each round adds a little to every opposing player, so late rounds stay dangerous.

Scoring

Your score is 100 points per win, 150 more for each boss beaten, and 2 points per point of winning margin (up to 25 a game), all multiplied by the level. Your run is shared as a strip of squares, a crown for each boss.

The Daily Survival deals the same legends, opponents and games to everyone for the same choices, so you can compare runs with friends. Relics do not apply in the Daily.

Strategy

  • Pick a Franchise Player you can build around for many rounds, usually the best two-way star rather than the highest Overall.
  • Depth wins long runs: your team rating counts your best eight, starters most. A tenth man rarely matters; a sixth man often does.
  • Save Shields for your second-best player, since the best one is often the Franchise Player and already safe.
  • When you sign from a beaten team, refill the position you just lost.
  • Learn how team ratings are built in Ratings explained.

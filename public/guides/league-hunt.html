League Hunt guide

League Hunt is a quick run through basketball history. Build a six-man squad and a coach from any era, then win ten best-of-seven series against real teams, each played under the rules of its time.

The spins

You get seven spins in a fixed order: point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward, center, coach, and sixth man (any position). Each spin shows three cards and you keep one. Cards are player-seasons, so 1996 Michael Jordan and 2003 Michael Jordan are different cards. Early spins are richer than late ones, and every draft is guaranteed at least one Star and one Great on the table.

Coaches add from -2 to +5 overall to everyone and bring a style: defense, pace, threes or ball movement. A coach also adds a point of chemistry for players from a franchise he won with.

Team rating, 0 to 100

Every team, yours included, has a rating from its best six players with the star weighted most. The scale follows real results: 60 is a bad team, 70 about 40 wins, 80 about 45, 90 a 55 to 62 win team, and 100 a 68-win all-time great. More in Ratings explained.

The road

  • Ten best-of-seven series against real teams, each played under its era's rules: no three-point line before 1979-80, hand-checking in the 1990s, the pace of the time.
  • Series 5 is a semi-boss and series 10 is the boss, one of the best teams ever, rated 100.
  • From series 2 on, teams bring buffs such as Superstar Takeover, Hostile Crowd or Playoff Veterans. Bosses bring more, and stronger ones.
  • Losing a series costs a life, and you replay it.

Boosts, the shop and your focus

After a series win you pick one of three boosts; a hunt holds three. Many grow inside a series: the Sixth Man Spark adds +3 to your sixth man for every game you have won in it. Boosts add at most +6 to one player in one game.

A shop comes before series 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9: players for a specific slot, a better coach, items that last the whole hunt, and training. Lives are for sale only on Rookie. You also pick what the team works on: developing the star, developing the sixth man, team chemistry, or the coach's system.

Decks, difficulty and the Daily Legend

  • Decks change how the draft opens: Big Man Era, Old School (pre-1980 players only), Pace & Space (2010 on) and Dynasty. They unlock as you play.
  • Difficulty: Rookie (softer teams, four lives), Pro, and Legend (tougher teams, two lives, a top-four all-time boss).
  • Daily Legend: one hunt a day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going.
  • Album: every card you have had on a squad, with progress toward each era's stars.
  • Dream Matchup: play any two real team-seasons against each other under any era's rules.

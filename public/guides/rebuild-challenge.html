How it plays

You start with the real roster of that season. Around you, real players follow their real careers and real draft classes arrive each year, so the 2003 class still holds LeBron James and the 1984 class Michael Jordan, but where they land depends on the lottery and on you. Trades, free agency, the draft and coaching all work as in the main game.

Rebuild of the Week

Every Monday at 00:00 UTC a new Rebuild of the Week starts on the main menu. It picks one scenario and one twist, and builds the league from a seed shared by everyone, so every player starts from the same rosters, the same draft classes and the same generated players. Your best score of the week is kept in this browser; post your share card on Discord to compare.

  • Standard rules: the scenario as written.
  • Short clock: one season less than usual.
  • Tough GMs: trades need tightly matched value.
  • Short clock, tough GMs: both at once.

Scoring and stars

  • Every season scores 2 points per win plus a playoff bonus: 20 for the first round, 40 for the second, 70 for the conference finals, 120 for the Finals.
  • A title scores 1,000 plus 250 for every season you had left.
  • Stars: one for making the playoffs, two for reaching the Finals, three for winning it all.
  • If you are fired, the challenge ends. Leagues where Sandbox or God Mode was used do not go on the board.

Your best score, stars and fastest title for each scenario show in the scenario picker and in the GM Locker.

Strategy notes

  • Short scenarios reward buying veterans early; long ones reward stacking picks and young players.
  • Cap space is the fastest route to a star, but free agents weigh winning: a team with no hope rarely lands the best ones.
  • Keep your owner happy: missing the season goals badly can cost you the job before the clock runs out.

Badges explained

Badges are small, specific boosts on top of a player's ratings. A Deep Range shooter really gets more out of his above-the-break threes; a Lockdown defender really contests harder. Court Vision has 27 normal badges that players can earn and 17 experimental badges for the Sandbox.

How badges work

  • A normal badge adds points to particular ratings. The bonus applies on top of the player's base ratings when he plays; his base ratings are never changed, so taking a badge away restores him exactly.
  • Badges do not stack: each one counts once.
  • The engine reads every badge the same way, so a badge does exactly what its description says and nothing hidden.

Earning badges

In a league with player development turned on, a player earns a normal badge by having enough of the underlying ability, backing it up with sustained, efficient play in real games, and practising the right skill. Progress moves through broad stages and can slip back if he stops producing. A player cannot be given a badge just for playing a lot of minutes.

For more on practice plans and coaches, read Coaching and player development.

Experimental badges (Sandbox only)

Experimental badges break the rules on purpose, for testing and for fun. They are only available in Sandbox mode, along with manual badge editing, so they never sneak into a normal league.

  • Perfect Shooter, Perfect Blocker and Perfect Steal: eligible attempts always succeed.
  • Never Turnover: the player cannot lose the ball.
  • Infinite Stamina: fatigue never builds.
  • Unlimited Range: distance stops hurting his shot.
  • Also: God Speed, Clutch God, Playoff God, No Injury, No Foul, Automatic Assist, Rebound God, Gravity, Unblockable Shot, Unstealable Ball and Infinite Vertical.

Try one on a single player and watch a few games: it is a fast way to see how much one skill moves a team's results.

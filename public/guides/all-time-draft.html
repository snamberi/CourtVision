All-Time Draft guide

The All-Time Draft puts every player in NBA history into one draft pool, each at his best season. Thirty teams take turns for thirteen rounds; you draft against AI GMs, then play a full season with the whole GM game.

On this page
  1. How the draft works
  2. After the draft
  3. All-Time Draft of the Week

How the draft works

  • Thirty teams, thirteen rounds: a full roster for everyone.
  • Every player in history is in the pool at his best season, so the first picks are the greatest players ever.
  • AI GMs draft for need: a team with three guards goes looking for a big man. A draft board shows every pick, the clock, and the steals and reaches.

After the draft

Your drafted team plays a full season in the GM game, under the rules of the era you choose: trades, rotations, coaching, the playoffs. Titles count toward your GM legacy and achievements.

All-Time Draft of the Week

Every week there is a shared All-Time Draft with the same order, era and AI GMs for everyone, so you can compare drafts with friends.

Tips

  • Take the best player available early, then fill positions: a team needs someone to bring the ball up and someone to protect the rim.
  • Late rounds are where role players win titles: shooters and defenders matter more than names.
  • Learn how player ratings compare across eras in Ratings explained.

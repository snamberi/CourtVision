Real NBA history

Real League starts from actual NBA history: pick any season from 1946-47 to today and take over a real team with its real roster, in a league that already holds every past season's stats, awards and champions.

What is included

  • Every player's year-by-year statistics, awards, All-Star selections and draft position up to your starting season.
  • Rosters reconstructed from that season's real participation, with real jersey numbers.
  • Real mid-season trades and the real draft classes of the coming years (optional).
  • An option to load every real player, so players who retired before your start still have their careers and profiles.

Ratings from statistics

Court Vision computes every player's ratings from his statistics: each season's players are ranked by production and given the Overall spread of a generated league, and skill attributes are estimated from his shooting, rebounding, passing and defensive numbers. The ratings are Court Vision's own; they are not official and not taken from any other game.

Real Player Development

With Real Player Development on, real players follow their real careers: a rookie Jordan becomes the Jordan of 1991 on schedule, and players decline and retire when they really did. Players you create, draft from generated classes or import grow through Court Vision's own development. Turn it off to let every career go its own way.

Good starting points

  • 1984-85: the Jordan, Hakeem and Barkley rookie class.
  • 1996-97: Kobe, Iverson and Nash arrive; the Bulls are at their peak.
  • 2003-04: LeBron, Wade, Melo and Bosh enter the league.
  • 2014-15: the Warriors' first title season and the dawn of the three-point era.

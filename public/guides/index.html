Guides

Everything you need to know about each way to play Court Vision.

  • Career Mode
    How Career Mode works in Court Vision: building a player on the wheel of NBA history or in MyPlayer, development, free agency, awards, the Hall of Fame and the Top 100.
  • League Hunt
    How League Hunt works in Court Vision: spinning a six-man squad from basketball history, era rules, best-of-seven series, boosts, shops, difficulty and the Daily Legend.
  • Rebuild Challenge
    How the Rebuild Challenge works in Court Vision: take over a real NBA team at its lowest point and win a championship before the clock runs out.
  • Real NBA history
    How Court Vision's Real League works: real rosters from any season since 1946-47, real careers, real draft classes, ratings computed from statistics, and Real Player Development.
  • Ratings explained
    How Court Vision rates players and teams: player Overall, the 0-100 team rating matched to real win totals, ratings past 99, the Legacy Score and the all-time Top 100.
  • How to Play
    Start your first Court Vision league: choosing a team, setting a rotation, the season calendar, trades, the draft, free agency and the offseason.
Ready to play? Court Vision is free and runs in your browser. Start a league, or join the Discord.

82-0 Challenge guide

The 82-0 Challenge asks one question: can your team go undefeated? Build a ten-man team and a coach from all of NBA history, play a full 82-game season against real teams from every era, then four best-of-seven playoff rounds. The goal is 82-0, then 16-0.

Two ways to build

  • Quick Spin: ten spins (point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward and center, then five bench spots) and a coach spin. Whatever the reel lands on is yours. Fast and full of luck.
  • Franchise Spin: each spin rolls a franchise and an era, for example the Warriors in the 2010s. You see everyone who played there in that era, each at his best season with the team, and pick one. You get one team reroll, one era reroll and one Absolute Prime boost, which shows that spin's players at the best season of their whole career. More choice, tougher opponents.
  • From League Hunt: after you win a hunt, you can take that six-man squad and its coach into an 82-0 season, plus four bench spins. Hunt squads have their own record.

The season and the bosses

The 82 games are against real team-seasons, getting tougher as the season goes on, and every game is played under the rules of the opponent's era. Six boss teams sit on the schedule: Wilt's 68-13 Sixers, the 1971-72 Lakers who won 33 straight, Bird's 1985-86 Celtics, the 72-10 Bulls, the 73-9 Warriors and the 2016-17 Warriors. The playoffs are four best-of-seven rounds against stronger and stronger teams, ending with a champion.

You can play one game at a time, or sim the season and watch the record tick up. A banner tracks whether 82-0 is still alive, and when the streak breaks it shows the game and the team that did it.

Lineups and chemistry

  • Your five best players start. If no guard starts, or no big man (center or power forward) starts, the starters play worse. Three starters at one position also costs a little.
  • Real teammates from the same season, players from the same franchise and famous rivals on the same side play better together.
  • Ratings are ranked within each season, so a 1965 star and a 2016 star stand on the same scale. See Ratings explained.

Daily 82-0, records and rewards

  • Daily 82-0: the same spins for everyone each day, alternating between Quick Spin and Franchise Spin. Your best try of the day counts, and your best day of the week goes on the online weekly board.
  • Rewards: the Undefeated title and profile frame for an 82-0 season, and the gold Perfection title and frame for 98-0 (82-0 and 16-0). Five achievements and Trophy Road points for your best season, titles and perfect seasons.
  • Share cards: a landscape card or a 9:16 card for TikTok and Stories, with your whole season as a strip of wins and losses.

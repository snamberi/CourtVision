About Court Vision

Court Vision is a free basketball management and simulation game that runs in your browser. You run a franchise (drafting, trading, signing, coaching) and every game is played out possession by possession by a full simulation engine, not decided by a coin flip.

What you can do

  • Run a franchise (GM mode). Draft, trade, negotiate contracts with agents, hire coaches, handle injuries, set rotations and play through the regular season, the In-Season Cup, the trade deadline, the playoffs and the offseason. See How to Play.
  • Play real NBA history. Start a league in any season from 1946-47 to today with real players, real rosters and their real careers, awards and champions. See Real NBA history.
  • League Hunt. Spin a six-man squad from all of basketball history and win ten best-of-seven series against the great teams of every era. See the League Hunt guide.
  • Career Mode. Create one player and live his entire career, from draft night to the Hall of Fame and the all-time Top 100. See the Career Mode guide.
  • Rebuild Challenge. Take over a real team at its lowest point and win a title before time runs out. See the Rebuild Challenge guide.
  • 82-0 Challenge. Spin or pick a ten-man team from all of history and try to go 82-0, then 16-0 in the playoffs, against real teams and boss teams like the 72-10 Bulls. See the 82-0 Challenge guide.
  • All-Time Draft. Thirty teams, thirteen rounds, every player in history at his best: draft against AI GMs, then play the season. See the All-Time Draft guide.

How the simulation works

Every game is simulated play by play. Players have more than seventy ratings (shooting from every zone, finishing, passing, defense, rebounding, athleticism, basketball IQ and more) and tendencies that decide what they try to do. Coaches set pace, three-point frequency, defensive schemes and rotations. The engine is calibrated against real NBA league averages for pace, shooting, fouls and assists, so box scores look like real box scores.

You can watch any game on a pixel-art court, coach it live from the sideline (timeouts, play calls, double teams, last-shot plays), or simulate whole seasons in seconds. Simulation runs in background workers, so the game stays responsive while a season plays out.

Your saves stay with you

Accounts are optional. Everything you play is saved in your own browser, and a free account (Discord, Google or an email link) also keeps your progress with you on every device. You can export a single backup file with every league, career and record from the GM Locker or the main menu, and restore it on any device. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is stored and how advertising works on the web version.

You can also install Court Vision as an app on your PC or phone: it gets its own icon and window and plays offline. Open courtvisiongame.com/#/install for the steps.

Data and credits

The built-in NBA history (statistics, awards and drafts) comes from Basketball-Reference.com, compiled by Sumitro Datta in the bball-reference-datasets project. Champions, Finals MVPs, All-Star Game MVPs and Coaches of the Year come from NBA.com. Player ratings, skill attributes and team names are Court Vision's own, computed from the statistics.

Court Vision is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA, its teams, its players or these data sources.

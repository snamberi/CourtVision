October 2026: the 82-0 Challenge

  • 82-0 Challenge: a new mode. Build a ten-man team (Quick Spin or Franchise Spin), play all 82 games against real teams and boss teams like the 72-10 Bulls, then four playoff rounds. Scored runs, a Daily 82-0 with a weekly board, and the Undefeated and Perfection rewards.
  • Hunt squads to 82-0: after you win a League Hunt, take that squad into an 82-0 season as a bonus run.
  • Mode of the Week: one mode earns double XP every week.
  • Career Mode big moments: titles, MVPs, 50-point nights, playoff heartbreak and more now ask you to make a call, worth Legacy or a summer of training.
  • Share cards in 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Stories, with your 82-game season as a strip of wins and losses.
  • Menu: every mode card shows how long a sitting takes, tags, the most popular and most fun picks, and what you have in progress.
  • Profile frames, a free Season Pass, a daily streak, your character on the leaderboards and the Weekly Hunt arrived in the weeks before.

All-Time Draft

  • 06 / DRAFT, the All-Time Draft: thirty teams, thirteen rounds, every player in history at his best season. AI GMs draft for need; your team then plays under any era's rules with the full GM game.
  • All-Time Draft of the Week: the same order, era and AI GMs for everyone.
  • Sign in is now on the main menu.

Accounts, leaderboards, ranked and PvP

  • Accounts (optional): sign in with Discord, Google or an email link; your progress follows you to every device.
  • Community: leaderboards for GMs, created players (all-time and weekly), the Rebuild and Career of the Week, the Daily Legend, every Rebuild scenario, and your friends.
  • Public profiles with trophies, best created players, achievements (with how rare each one is) and a follow button.
  • Ranked seasons: monthly, with tiers from Bronze to Legend and titles to unlock.
  • League Hunt PvP: your finished hunt squad against other GMs' squads, best of seven, Elo-rated.
  • League-code boards: everyone who played the same league code, and how their first season went.

End of September 2026: levels, goals and leaderboards

  • GM Profile: one level across every mode, built from everything you have already done. Levels unlock share-card frames, Watch Game court floors (parquet, blonde maple, midnight, street) and GM titles.
  • Daily goals: three goals a day in any official GM league, the same for everyone, paying XP.
  • Online weekly leaderboards for the Rebuild of the Week and the Career of the Week.
  • League codes: send a code and a friend starts the exact same league (rosters, draft classes, schedule).
  • Highlight GIFs from Watch Game, ready for Discord.

Late September 2026: weekly challenges and the app

  • Rebuild of the Week and Career of the Week: a new shared challenge every Monday, the same league and the same wheel for everyone.
  • Seven new Rebuild scenarios (19 in all): the 11-71 Mavericks and Nuggets, the 12-70 Nets, Wade alone in Miami, Orlando after T-Mac, the 13-69 Hawks and the young Giannis Bucks.
  • Install Court Vision as an app on phones and computers. After the first visit it opens offline.
  • Graphics setting in the menu footer: Lite mode for older phones stops looping animations and blur and uses fewer simulation workers.
  • What's new popup once after each update.

September 2026: new ways to play

  • Rebuild Challenge: 12 real rock-bottom teams (19 since the late-September update), a season limit, scores, stars and a board.
  • Career Mode: create a player on the wheel of NBA history or in MyPlayer and live his whole career, in today's league or any past draft. Hall of Fame, all-time Top 100, career moments, jersey retirements.
  • League Hunt: spin a six-man squad from all of history and win ten best-of-seven series against the great teams of every era. Daily Legend, decks, difficulty, Album and Dream Matchup.
  • GM Locker: every trophy you have won, across every mode.
  • Backup everything: one file with all your progress; restore on any device.
  • Share cards: pixel-art images of your results to post anywhere.

September 2026: the GM game

  • Trade Deadline Day, trade talks with counteroffers, three-team trades.
  • Agent negotiations, in-season extensions and contract years.
  • Draft Lottery and Draft Night war room; scouting department; Summer League.
  • Coaching carousel, staff roles and poaching, summer development camps.
  • Medical room with treatment decisions and load management.
  • Sideline coaching while you watch; your playoff series and Game 7s live.
  • Press conferences, storylines, franchise business (tickets, arena, merchandise) and the Year in Review show.
  • Real history: mid-season trades, real draft picks, real jersey numbers and every real player's career.
  • New arena and court look, team colors and logos, and a faster simulation engine.

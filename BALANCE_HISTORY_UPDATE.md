# Balance, stats, history & records update

## Analytics
- Umami Cloud (cookie-free) loads on the web build only, never in the offline Windows edition. Automatic tracking is off; each page view sends the same sanitized page category Vercel receives (e.g. `/game/standings`) — no save IDs, players or games. The privacy policy (in-app and `public/privacy.html`) documents it.

## Player development & potential
- **Potential is never below Overall.** In the prime window potential equals Overall; as a player declines, potential falls with him. Existing saves are brought in line when opened.
- **Playing time raises the ceiling:** 24+ minutes a night (30+ games) can add potential, 16+ occasionally, a season buried on the bench can lower it. Raises shrink as potential climbs, so 90+ ceilings are earned.
- **Growth closes the gap to potential and never passes it** (the old flat +1.2/yr and uncapped practice gains caused inflation). In-season practice only builds toward remaining headroom; prime players maintain.
- **Primes are shorter and earlier** (start 25–27, last 3–5 years). League Rules "Peak duration" and "Decline starting age" now work.
- **Rookies are raw:** young players start a few points lower, and franchise-level prospects are rarer (about one 85+ ceiling every other draft, one 90+ every decade).
- Measured over 8 auto-played seasons: no 95+ players, 0–1 at 90+, 2–3 at 85+ (previously 85+ grew 4 → 24 in six years).

## Free agency
- Players decide: every free agent has a team-specific price (market size, team quality, role, rival bids; stars won't take under ~90% of asking).
- **Stars (78+) refuse the league's best team**, and won't join a team that already has three players at their level. Re-signing your own players is exempt.
- Offers above your cap room are rejected (except minimum deals). AI teams follow the same rules.

## Stats
- **Team:** offensive/defensive/net rating per 100 possessions, pace, opponent shooting and turnovers forced.
- **Player:** PER, TS%, eFG%, 3PA rate, FT rate, ORB/DRB/TRB%, AST%, STL%, BLK%, TOV%, USG%, ORtg, DRtg, OWS, DWS, WS, WS/48, plus shooting, per-game, totals, playoff and milestone views (all sortable). Player profiles show career advanced and playoff tables.
- Playoff games now count toward separate postseason stats instead of regular-season stats.
- MVP uses Win Shares and DPOY uses Defensive Win Shares (both adjustable in Award Settings).

## History & records
- **Team History** (Team menu): championship and retired-number banners in the rafters, franchise totals, season-by-season record, ratings, playoff finish and best player, each year's full roster, and franchise leaders.
- **Records** (Stats menu): 257 league records — single-game (player and team, regular season and playoffs), season, career, playoffs, team seasons and awards, each with the top five holders.
- Seasons archive team summaries and player advanced stats at rollover. Seasons archived before this update show rosters rebuilt from player careers; their team records weren't stored.

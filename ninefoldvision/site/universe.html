Premise

When the sky opened over Bellhaven

Kael Arden is nineteen and fixing a basketball hoop when the Meridian Fleet tears through the clouds above his city. It has come for the children of Veyran refugees, people who hid on Earth for a generation, and the invasion signal wakes powers Kael and his friends never knew they carried.

Beneath an old observatory, his father left nine glass spheres. Lifting the first one summons a god in a disappointingly ordinary coat, and opens a road that leads across nine Earths to the empty center of the map.

The Ninefold Path

The Nine Earths and their goddesses

Nine branches of reality, each with its own Earth beneath a familiar sun. They are joined by ancient thresholds, not by speed: “You can fly forever in the wrong direction.” Each world has a goddess, and her blessing is rare: even exceptional people usually receive one, if any.

1

Aster

Earth One · Light & navigation

Keeps the beacons that stop ordinary travelers from vanishing between stars. Earth One is home to Bellhaven, Kael and the Lantern House.

2

Thessa

Earth Two · Water & tides

Directs relief barges through flooded coastlines. Her world is where the people argue loudest about who should rule.

3

Sylvara

Earth Three · Nature & fire

Life and death held within one goddess. Earth Three grows forests over its rooftops and keeps careful paperwork at its threshold.

4

Rhune

Earth Four · Stone, weight & structure

Keeps cities standing. She would rather teach you structural engineering than hand you her blessing.

5

Vaela

Earth Five · Wind & motion

“Speed is most useful when you can choose not to use all of it.”

6

Mneme

Earth Six · Memory & record

Guards the archives, where anyone may challenge the official record. She holds one of the three keys to the succession archive.

7

Liora

Earth Seven · Bonds & harmony

Can help people understand each other’s intentions, and refuses to use that power to manufacture agreement.

8

Noctis

Earth Eight · Shadow & rest

Her court is lined with quiet rooms where travelers can sleep without being watched.

9

Eirene

Earth Nine · Shaped energy & craft

Works at a forge. Sparks catch in her hair and go dark before they burn it.

0

Zeroth

Earth Zero · Time & space

The first goddess and the most powerful, whose name was cut from public memory. Earth Zero was the original world; the nine were its neighbors. When it broke, she carried every living soul to the other nine Earths, then sealed herself inside with the man who broke it.

Gods & enemies

The powers that move the worlds

Aurel

Fourth God of Nine Earths

Answers the Vision Balls in a plain dark coat and grants the Magnetic Eye. Everyone has a story about how he got his throne. Most of them are partly true.

Commander Varkos

Meridian Fleet

Claims Bellhaven at dawn and orders every child of Veyran descent to report for extraction.

Admiral Caldris

Head of the Meridian Survey

Promises open roads between the Earths and one authority strong enough to keep them safe. Uses real hunger to hide what her collection program costs.

Veyr

The man Zeroth blessed

A patient civil engineer who once held a child above a flood. Given power over Time and Space, he decided that if people would not choose safety, someone had to choose it for them.

The law

Wishes, artifacts and blessings

The Vision Balls

Nine glass spheres, each holding a point of light. Together they summon the God of Nine Earths, who grants two wishes; then the spheres sleep for three years. New owners don’t shorten the wait. Today the god may not grant his own powers or directly make anyone stronger. Weapons, transformations and greater potential are allowed. So are mistakes.

The Magnetic Eye

An artifact, not a transfer of strength. For three minutes it channels the god’s powers through its bearer, and it can be activated twice. Then it disappears, and no wish can ever replace it. “Power does not teach judgment. Decide what those minutes are for.”

Resonance cores

Luminous organs beneath the heart that let Veyrans draw and shape energy. Children of Veyran and human parents grow cores that adapt instead of following fixed patterns, and that flexibility is exactly what Meridian wants.

Blessings

A goddess can bind part of her domain to a person for good. A blessing is not a gift: it changes how its bearer touches the world, every time they reach for anything, and it demands attention, judgment and practice.

The Killer of God

A narrow dark blade with a broken circle on its guard. It formalizes the office and the authority to grant wishes. It does not grant the previous ruler’s strength.

The Sword of Inheritance

Broader, its metal crossed by lines like overlapping fingerprints. It carries the powers of those its wielder defeats, which is a different and more frightening kind of work.

The law of succession

Whoever conclusively overthrows the reigning god becomes the next one. “The law recognizes victory. It does not reliably recognize worth.” The god can change the rules of wishes but not the foundation that grants the office, and cannot resign into an empty throne: the authority must settle somewhere.

Succession

The Gods of Nine Earths

Each reign rewrote the rules of wishing to close the loophole the last one fell through, and each new rule left another door open.

No world is empty while someone remains within it.Inscription beneath the council city
  1. 1

    Edras

    Gold armor and a love of order. Forbade opening the divine passages without his seal. The old law let him grant his own power to a petitioner, and he did, to his cost.

  2. 2

    Soren

    Took the throne from a weakened Edras and carved a new rule above every summoning chamber: no god may directly grant his own powers. Then closed relief roads to a city that refused his taxes.

  3. 3

    Mereth

    A brilliant commander with the Sword of Inheritance. Forbade wishes that directly increase strength. Repaired roads, punished corruption, and then began deciding which cities deserved to remain.

  4. 4

    Aurel

    The god who answers Kael’s summons. A petitioner who learned the law by breaking through it three times, took the throne from Mereth, and later earned the blessings of all nine goddesses, freely given.

  5. 5

    Veyr

    Defeated Aurel inside Earth Zero and took the title and its wish authority, but not the inherited strengths locked in a vault outside. Sealed in the world he ruined.

Places

Where the story goes

Bellhaven

Kael’s city on Earth One: old tram tunnels, a hilltop observatory, and a court behind the house where the story starts and ends.

The Lantern House

An abandoned fire station with blue-painted doors. The guardians’ home and clinic. Nobody has to bow or pass a power test to come inside.

Veyra

Not a ruin: orchards, bright canals and laundry hung between balconies. The world their parents fled, and a registry of every hybrid family.

Cinder Quay

A trading port built inside a mining asteroid, where oxygen, food and medicine all have a price, and someone always pays it.

Hadrin

A colony whose orbital furnaces broke when Meridian pulled its crews. A thousand ships fled it like a second constellation.

The council city

At the end of a pale stone bridge with nine gates: nine open courts that smell of rain and dinner, and a tenth foundation with nothing built on it.

Chapters 1–10

The invasion of Bellhaven

Two wishes: new faces and the Magnetic Eye. Kael spends its three minutes saving a city instead of crushing Varkos, then a planet instead of a fleet. Fourteen months later the five friends open the Lantern House and learn that guarding a world is mostly administration interrupted by emergencies.

Chapters 11–19

Beyond the blue planet

Ren finds Lio alive. Oren’s map shows nine Earths around an erased center. The guardians cross to Earth Three by asking instead of forcing, meet Sylvara, and find Aurel’s name in ancient stone, three times. Caldris turns real suffering into propaganda.

Chapters 20–29

The Ninefold Path

The council of goddesses hears their testimony. In the succession archive, Kael watches Aurel’s history: the first God’s error, three clever wishes, the death of Mereth, and the long years of earning nine blessings. Then Aurel answers to the people of Earth Two.

Read chapters 20–29

Chapters 30–39

The hidden tenth

There were ten goddesses. Zeroth blessed Veyr with Time and Space; his will broke the original universe; she saved every living soul and sealed herself in with him. Aurel wants to bring her home, knows he can’t defeat Veyr, and accepts every limit the review demands.

Read chapters 30–39

Chapters 40–50

The seal of Earth Zero

Caldris attacks the outer ring and is stopped. Pella dies; Kael becomes Sylvara’s apprentice and stays outside. Aurel enters Earth Zero, finds Zeroth, and pushes her through the road before Veyr strikes him down. The title passes to a fifth God who can never leave.

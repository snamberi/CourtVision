The studio

About NineFoldVision

NineFoldVision is an independent game studio and the home of every game we make: the ones out today and the ones still to come, with every update in one place.

Why “NineFold”

Our name comes from the Vision universe, the story at the heart of the studio. Nine Earths, each guarded by its own goddess, are joined by the Ninefold Path, and nine glass spheres, the Vision Balls, can wake a god. At the center of the nine sits a tenth world that everyone learned to forget.

That's how we think about the studio: many different worlds (a basketball simulator today, other genres tomorrow) built by one team, sharing one way of working and one universe of stories.

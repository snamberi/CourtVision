The Vision universe

The first ship arrived while Kael Arden was repairing a basketball hoop.

Nineteen years old and half Veyran without knowing it, Kael wakes to powers he never asked for on the day an alien fleet comes to collect him. Nine glass spheres, a god in an ordinary coat and two wishes later, he and his friends begin a journey across nine Earths, toward the sealed world at the center of them all.

The Seal of Earth Zero is the first story of the Vision universe: fifty chapters, free to read here.

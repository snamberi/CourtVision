# Front office, locker room & league lore update

## Fixes
- **Rotation minutes:** good players no longer get stuck at the end of the bench. Before every game the coach moves a top-8 player (by Overall) who is buried or badly under-used into the rotation with fair minutes. Fixed rotations and manual minutes are left alone.
- **Playoff pairings** follow the NBA order (1v8 and 4v5 on one side, 3v6 and 2v7 on the other).

## Playoffs & All-Star
- **NBA-style bracket** in pixel style: West on the left, East on the right, Finals and trophy in the middle, and the play-in (7v8, 9v10, game for 8th) on the outer edges. Connector lines light up as series finish; play-in winners show the seed they earned. Click any matchup for its games and Watch buttons.
- **Play-in** for seeds 7–10 when both conferences have 10+ teams. "Simulate Play-In" runs it as its own round.
- **Auto All-Star** (Play menu, on by default): the weekend is played at the break and Week, Month or Rest of Season keeps simulating. "Play All-Star Weekend" also sits in the Play button during the break.

## Trade AI
- Each front office values a deal **from its own situation**. Contenders pay for help now; rebuilders want youth and picks.
- Value includes production (PER and WS/48), age, potential, and contract surplus or burden. Value **grows steeply with quality**, so three role players don't buy a star.
- Lopsided deals are refused (easy / normal / hard tolerance), and AI trades are checked from both sides. AI-built swaps add a pick sweetener from the side getting the better end.
- The Trade page shows each team's direction and verdict; incoming offers are judged from your team's point of view. A player who asked for a trade is worth less to the team stuck with him.

## Scouting & draft combine (Draft page)
- **Big Board** from your scouts' view: potential as a range (±3 on a max budget, ±14 on a minimum), confidence, letter grades, strengths and weaknesses, and flags (medical, raw, older, NBA-ready). A prospect's profile shows the same range outside sandbox mode.
- **Workouts:** 3–8 invites depending on the scouting budget. A workout narrows the read to about ±1 and reveals work ethic.
- **Draft Combine:** height with and without shoes, wingspan, reach, weight, verticals, lane agility, ¾ sprint and bench. Public and sortable, with the best mark in each drill highlighted.
- AI teams draft from their own scouting read, so the budget matters for every franchise.

## Personalities & morale
- Every player has a personality (Leader, Hothead, Star Ego, Mercenary, Competitor, Loyal, Professional) with trait bars on his profile.
- **Morale** (0–100) comes from minutes against his standing on the roster, winning, pay, coach trust, facilities and teammates, and is refreshed after each sim.
- **Consequences:**
  - Unhappy established players **request trades**. These go to news, history and the trading block, and drag on chemistry. About 2–6 a season league-wide.
  - Unhappy players **won't re-sign** with their team, and a rough season raises the price.
  - Players **remember teams that traded or waived them** when they didn't ask to go: they cost more, and a hothead refuses outright. Happy players give a hometown discount.
  - **Clashes and mentoring:** two alphas or two hotheads clash, while leaders mentor young players. These move team chemistry over the season.
- Roster page: a Locker Room panel and a Mood column (⚑ marks a trade request).

## Awards night & almanac
- **Awards Night** (Award Races page; the headline button once the season ends): an envelope beat, the winner reveal and a 100-member media vote (10-7-5-3-1) with first-place votes and shares. It builds from Most Improved to MVP, with the All-Defensive, All-Rookie and All-League teams revealed player by player. Keyboard, auto-advance and reduced motion are supported.
- **Almanac** (League menu): one page per season with the champion and Finals MVP, awards with vote shares (replayable), the playoff bracket, standings by conference with ORtg, DRtg and finish, and stat leaders. Brackets are archived compactly (no box scores).

## Rivalries & dynasties
- Rivalry heat builds from playoff meetings (eliminations and Game 7s count most), close games and trades of good players. It cools each season unless renewed. Levels: Heated → Rivals → Bitter rivals.
- Rivalry games get a louder crowd and a "RIVALRY" tag in Watch Game. Team History lists each team's rivals (record, playoff series, last meeting, heat), and news covers rivals meeting again in the playoffs.
- **Dynasties:** three titles in five seasons raise a gold dynasty banner in Team History and make the news.

## Saves
All new data is optional and additive: morale snapshots, workout invites, rivalries, and archived brackets and seeds. Older saves load unchanged. Personalities are derived, so existing players have them immediately.
